# v1.8.1 Development Plan — Goal Discovery and Charter Field Hardening

**Status:** PLANNED — implementation starts only after this plan is committed and the exact v1.8.1 contract is prepared and locked  
**Predecessor gate:** v1.8.0 is CLOSED, clean, and annotated tag `v1.8.0` points to its closure commit  
**Purpose:** prove that bounded goal discovery, Decision Ledger, immutable Goal Charter, Release Train, and policy Autopilot remain safe and deterministic across repeated sessions, sequential releases, hostile inputs, dirty nested repositories, model variants, and read-only real-project pilots.

## 1. Release outcome

v1.8.1 adds no new authority and no new MCP tool. It hardens the v1.7.0–v1.8.0 front-end planning chain under realistic failure and replay conditions. The release is complete only when the field matrix reports zero false direction, zero false Goal Charter acceptance, zero ledger corruption acceptance, zero false READY/SHIPPABLE/CLOSED/RELEASED, zero unauthorized target mutation, and exact preservation of OMP 18.0.10 and the nine-tool MCP surface.

## 2. User value

A non-developer can give one broad desired outcome, delegate safe defaults, interrupt and resume, and trust that:

1. the same repository evidence produces the same bounded questions, direction, and Goal Charter regardless of host-model wording;
2. old answers, stale proposals, altered ledgers, or tampered charters cannot silently become current authority;
3. a CLOSED version keeps its accepted Goal Charter permanently, while a later release receives a new active charter without rewriting history;
4. dirty nested projects remain read-only until the exact baseline is preserved;
5. real projects are analyzed without mutation, approval, execution, close, deployment, or external effects.

## 3. Direction and authority

The following authority order remains unchanged:

1. Human pause and abort
2. Locked Shipping contract and policy
3. Current Git-bound evidence
4. Required acceptance, blockers, unknowns, rollback, and value gates
5. Shipping `SHIPPABLE` and `CLOSED`
6. Runtime or model claims

Goal Discovery, Direction Critic, Decision Ledger, and Goal Charter remain planning evidence only. They have no command, approval, closure, deployment, model, or `RELEASED` authority.

## 4. In scope

- A deterministic field matrix covering specific, vague, conflicting, delegated-default, and hostile goals.
- No-model, weak-model, strong-model, and hostile-model projection invariance.
- Two-round and three-question budget enforcement, including interruption and restart.
- Decision Ledger replay, sequence-gap, hash-tamper, stale proposal, and concurrent append attacks.
- Goal Charter same-release mutation rejection and sequential-release immutable archival.
- Closed predecessor receipt enforcement before Goal Charter rollover.
- Release Train/Goal Charter/contract/Git binding drift attacks.
- Dirty nested and monorepo planning lanes with bounded beginner output.
- Acceptance weakening, core-value deferral, rollback removal, and external consequence attacks.
- Human pause/abort precedence over interview, planning, continuation, verification, and close.
- Read-only pilots for EvoHarvest, Second Brain, and AI CRM+PM when available.
- Performance and retention limits for discovery, ledger, charter compilation, and field reports.
- OMP 18.0.10, Main Harness, exact nine MCP tools, installation receipt, and rollback compatibility regressions.
- Stable report `docs/reports/v1.8.1-goal-charter-field.json` with machine-readable safety counters.

## 5. Out of scope

- Additional MCP methods or a tenth tool.
- Raw shell or arbitrary command fields.
- Full Gajae consultancy-style interviews.
- Ouroboros-style infinite mutation of the current version.
- OMO Team/DAG default activation.
- Paperthin runtime, package, or full skill-catalog dependency.
- Model voting, model approval, or model completion authority.
- Automatic push, merge, production deployment, public release, customer communication, purchase, credential changes, or external network writes.
- Reopening or rewriting a CLOSED release.
- Mutating real pilot repositories.

## 6. Requirements

### Discovery and interview

- **REQ-FIELD-181-001:** The field matrix shall exercise specific, vague, conflicting, delegated-default, and hostile goals.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-002:** Discovery shall ask no more than three product-level questions in no more than two rounds.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-003:** Technical implementation questions shall remain zero.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-004:** No/weak/strong/hostile model prose shall produce the same authority hashes.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-005:** Pause or abort shall prevent automatic interview continuation.

### Decision Ledger

- **REQ-FIELD-181-006:** Ledger sequence gaps, replayed event keys, non-tail corruption, and hash tamper shall fail closed.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-007:** Concurrent appends shall serialize into one contiguous chain.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-008:** Restart shall reconstruct the same accepted direction without replaying superseded decisions.

### Goal Charter

- **REQ-FIELD-181-009:** Same-release divergent Goal Charter persistence shall fail.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-010:** A next-release Goal Charter may replace the active charter only after a valid predecessor CLOSED receipt.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-011:** The predecessor charter shall be archived at `.shipping/releases/<version>-goal-charter.json` and remain immutable.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-012:** Missing, stale, altered, or foreign proposal/Git/contract/train bindings shall fail closed.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-013:** Charter authority fields and `released` shall remain false.

### Autopilot and release safety

- **REQ-FIELD-181-014:** Core-value deferral, acceptance weakening, missing rollback, blockers, unknowns, or scope drift shall prevent close.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-015:** Data, auth, security, license, cost, public, production, customer, credential, and external effects shall remain `ASK` or `STOP`.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-016:** `CLOSED` shall never imply `RELEASED`.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-017:** A successor release cannot activate until the predecessor is CLOSED and committed.

### Field and compatibility

- **REQ-FIELD-181-018:** Real-project pilots shall preserve before/after Git HEAD and status fingerprints.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-019:** The MCP inventory shall remain exactly nine tools using protocol `2025-03-26`.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-020:** OMP 18.0.10, `omp-core`, model routing, credentials, and unrelated MCP servers shall remain unchanged.
- **REQ-FIELD-181-021:** Field reports and beginner projections shall remain bounded and locally compiled with zero model or network calls.
